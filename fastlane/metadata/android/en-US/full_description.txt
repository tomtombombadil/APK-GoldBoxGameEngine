<p>Play SSI's Gold Box <i>Advanced Dungeons &amp; Dragons</i> games on your phone, tablet or Android handheld.</p>
<ul>
<li>Pool of Radiance</li>
<li>Curse of the Azure Bonds</li>
<li>Secret of the Silver Blades</li>
<li>Pools of Darkness</li>
<li>Gateway to the Savage Frontier</li>
<li>Treasures of the Savage Frontier</li>
<li>Champions of Krynn</li>
<li>Death Knights of Krynn</li>
<li>The Dark Queen of Krynn</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Bring your own game.</b> Nothing from the games comes with the app: works from the original game files that you supply from your own copies of the games (GOG or Steam releases), copied onto the device.</p>

<p>This app uses the open-source CYD-Gold-Box-Game-Engine and parts of the PortMaster port for retro handhelds (all by the same author) to build a wonderful Android experience for these classic games.</p>
<ul>
<li>Every game's journal, made from your copy's own journal (PDF) and shown when the game mentions an entry</li>
<li>the Companion: the area map and the party at a glance beside the game</li>
<li>Quick Actions, the AD&amp;D 1E rules switches, Game Fixes, optional cheats, the original music</li>
<li>Controls any way you like: a Bluetooth or USB controller, on-screen buttons, or touch on every screen</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Copy your Steam/GOG game files from your PC into a folder of your choise on your device. (Recommend: GoldBox\games) Then point the app to that folder. The app asks for All Files Access to read that folder; it reads and writes only there. Or use the app's private folder (Internal storage › Android\data\io.github.tomtombombadil.goldbox\files\games), and you don't need to give the app any special permissions.</p>
